---
title: "Weary"
date: 2005-07-17 02:30:00 -0500

content_author: nehar
content_date: July 17, 2005

categories: mine
---

I have spent too much blood on this, <br>
This scent of a lingering pain <br>
that so much catharsis hasn't washed away. <br>
Visions and love I could speak of,<br>
but to what end?<br>
<br>
If you could just see, for a moment, how I see<br>
I would show you a thing so tender and so true,<br>
That you'd know belief again, and pain.<br>
<br>
In every moment that we rebuild our certainties with the breath we draw,<br>
I consecrate another brick to this.<br>
<br>
But what could I build?<br>
to what purpose?<br>
How could I keep the soft summer rain<br>
from washing away all that stands?<br>
<br>
And I am weary. too much blood.<br>
Too much that must be given, again and again<br>
for an edifice built on sand.<br>
